AI vs Human: Color Game

Mix cyan, magenta, yellow and black to match the target color. Get under 5% error before the Bayesian Optimization AI does.

How to play
  • Adjust the CMYK sliders, then hit Submit to make a guess. You only see the color after you submit.
  • Every time you guess, the AI makes one guess too.
  • You and the AI both start with the same 8 random reference colors (at the bottom).
  • No internet help, and no starting a new game to dodge a bad attempt!
